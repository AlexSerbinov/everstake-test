# Findings documents shown by the app

These four Markdown documents are the Ukrainian bodies of the Findings page. Their [English counterparts](en/README.md) are in `en/`. They are committed explanatory content, not generated JavaScript or private user data.

| Document | Subject |
|---|---|
| [insurance-scope.md](insurance-scope.md) | Insurance coverage and its stated limits |
| [nist-certification.md](nist-certification.md) | Certification terminology and supporting evidence |
| [principal-risk.md](principal-risk.md) | Principal-risk statements and their scope |
| [tax-reporting.md](tax-reporting.md) | Tax-reporting information and gaps |

[The Findings feature](../../web/features/findings/README.md) fetches and displays the selected document; its download uses the same body. Titles and language choices come from [catalog.ts](../../web/features/findings/catalog.ts). Keep dates, source links and limitations aligned between language versions. These documents describe their recorded research, not freshly verified current facts.
